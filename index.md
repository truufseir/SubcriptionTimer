Privacy Policy
Effective Date: October 20, 2026

Last Updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Subscription Timer ("we," "us," or "our") handles user information when you use our mobile application (the "Application").

We believe in data minimization. The Application is built with a local-first design: your records remain on your device, and we do not operate proprietary user accounts, sell personal data, or maintain cloud databases for behavioral profiling. When you request remote alert delivery, message routing is handled by an enterprise messaging processor as described below.

1. Information Collection and Storage
When configuring your alert preferences within the Application, you may voluntarily input:

Contact Data: An email address and/or mobile telephone number.

Subscription Records: Names of service providers, billing dates, renewal cycles, and cancellation deadlines.

Localized Device Storage
All core subscription logs, renewal schedules, and contact credentials you enter are stored locally within your device's private application sandbox.

We do not maintain independent proprietary web servers or database clusters to archive your ongoing subscription records.

We do not correlate your contact details with advertising identifiers, device fingerprints, or third-party tracking software.

2. Purpose and Use of Information
Your contact credentials and alert schedules are processed strictly for utility-focused transactional functions:

Reminder Dispatch: Routing scheduled free trial expiration, cancellation, and renewal notices to your designated email address or mobile phone number.

Service Execution: Triggering timed alert payloads configured by your local device timer.

We do not use your contact information to:

Send unsolicited promotional, affiliate, or marketing communications.

Build consumer advertising profiles.

Sell, lease, or trade personal data to third-party data brokers.

3. Third-Party Transmission & Data Processing (Brevo Integration)
While your data originates locally, the Application does not host its own email or cellular telecom servers. To deliver outbound Email and SMS alerts to your device, the Application transmits destination credentials via an API integration to our transactional messaging infrastructure partner:

Third-Party Messaging Processor (Brevo):

We utilize Brevo (formerly Sendinblue, operated by Brevo SAS, France) as our technical data processor to execute outbound SMS and transactional email deliveries.

Data Transmitted to Brevo:

When an alert triggers, the recipient endpoint (your email address or phone number) and the specific notification message body (e.g., your subscription reminder) are transmitted to Brevo solely for routing and delivery.

Processor Obligations & Security:

Brevo processes your transmission data as a Data Processor pursuant to strict contractual Data Processing Agreements (DPA) under European GDPR and international data protection standards. Brevo employs encrypted transmission protocols (TLS/SSL) and secure data centers. Brevo does not sell your contact information to third parties, nor are they authorized to use your recipient data for independent marketing.

In-App Purchases: Upgrades to the Pro version ($5) are executed directly via Google Play In-App Billing or Apple In-App Purchase. We never receive, process, or retain credit card numbers, bank details, or financial billing information.

4. Data Retention and Deletion
On Your Device: You can update or delete your email, phone number, and subscription entries directly in the Application settings at any time. Clearing the Application cache/data or uninstalling the app permanently purges all locally stored records.

With Brevo (Transmission Logs): Transactional transmission logs (such as message delivery receipts, bounce statuses, and timestamps) are retained ephemerally on Brevo’s secure servers strictly as necessary to verify message delivery, troubleshoot failed transmissions, and prevent network abuse (e.g., spam and fraud prevention), in accordance with Brevo's standard retention schedules and statutory obligations.

5. Children's Online Privacy
The Application does not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13 (or 16 within the European Economic Area). Because configuration is restricted to local input on the host device, parents or guardians retain complete control over device access and app removal.

6. Security Safeguards
We protect your data by eliminating centralized databases of user subscription histories. Data stored on your physical device is protected by your operating system’s sandbox encryption. Data transmitted off-device for alert routing via Brevo’s API is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS protocols.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in technical infrastructure, legal requirements, or operating system guidelines. Any updates will be published to this URL with a revised "Last Updated" date.
