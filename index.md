Privacy Policy
Effective Date: October 20, 2026

Last Updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Subscription Timer ("we," "us," or "our") handles user information when you use our mobile application (the "Application").

We are committed to user privacy. We design our software on a "local-first" architecture: we do not maintain external user accounts, and we do not store, harvest, sell, or transmit your personal data to remote servers for profiling or marketing purposes.

1. Information Collection and Storage
When configuring alert preferences within the Application, you may voluntarily input:

Contact Data: An email address and/or mobile telephone number.

Subscription Records: Names of service providers, billing dates, renewal cycles, and cancellation deadlines.

Localized Device Storage
All information entered into the Application—including your email address, phone number, and subscription logs—is stored exclusively in your device's local application sandbox/internal storage.

We do not operate proprietary cloud databases to archive your personal contact credentials.

Your data is not linked to cross-device identity trackers or advertising identifiers.

2. Purpose and Use of Information
Your contact credentials are used strictly for transactional, utility-based alert functionality:

Reminder Dispatch: Directing scheduled cancellation, free trial expiry, or renewal alerts to you via Email or SMS.

Service Delivery: Providing timely push or automated reminders configured by your local device timer settings.

We do not use your phone number or email address to:

Send promotional or third-party marketing materials.

Build consumer profiles or run behavioral analytics.

Sell, license, or broker your personal data to data brokers or advertising networks.

3. Third-Party Transmission & Gateway Services
The Application does not maintain an independent backend database. However, to physically deliver off-device notifications (such as an outbound SMS or transactional email alert), the Application or its designated delivery relay must transmit destination credentials to standard infrastructure service providers:

Transactional Telephony/Email Relays: If SMS or email alerts are routed via industry-standard delivery platforms (e.g., Twilio, AWS SES, or standard device-level system SMS intents), your phone number or email is transmitted solely to execute message delivery.

Zero Secondary Use: Any third-party delivery infrastructure utilized processes transmission requests on an ephemerally transactional basis and is contractually or legally barred from using recipient details for independent purposes.

In-App Purchases (Google Play / App Store): Upgrades to the Pro version ($5) are handled directly through Google Play In-App Billing or Apple In-App Purchase. We never receive, store, or process your credit card, debit card, or financial billing information.

4. Data Retention, Deletion, and User Control
Because all records are stored locally on your device:

Modification: You can edit or delete your email address, phone number, or alert entries at any time directly within the Application's settings screen.

Permanent Deletion: Clearing the Application's cache/data via your mobile operating system settings, or uninstalling the Application entirely, will permanently and irrecoverably erase all stored contact details and subscription data from your device.

5. Children's Online Privacy
The Application does not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). Since data is restricted to local storage on the host device, parents and guardians maintain complete control over data entry and app removal.

6. Security Safeguards
We protect your data by eliminating centralized database vulnerabilities: because we do not centralize or store your data on external company servers, your contact details cannot be compromised through breaches of our systems. Protection of data on the device itself is governed by your operating system’s encryption and security standards.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We reserve the right to amend this Privacy Policy to reflect updates in legal requirements, operating system compliance standards, or feature adjustments. Any modifications will be posted to this public URL with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
